Fruit Bowl

Whether its the peak heat months of the summer or the cozy cold days of winter, this recipe is sure to provide you with the nutrients you need to boost you immune system and fight off toxins.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 small watermelon cut into small cubes
  • 1 pineapple peeled and cut into small pieces
  • 3 cups seedless red grapes rinsed and halved
  • 1 cup cantaloupe peeled and diced
  • 1 pint fresh strawberries washed, hulled, and halved
  • 3 cups mixed berries blueberries, raspberries, or blackberries, rinsed
  • 3 kiwis peeled and sliced

Steps

  1. Place all the cut fruits in a large serving bowl.
  2. Toss gently to combine.
  3. Serve immediately, or cover and chill briefly if desired.
