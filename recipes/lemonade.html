Lemonade

Description

The famous quincher of thirst from a hot summer day. This natural ingredient recipe is one thats sure to appease your families delite. We included measurements to accomodate a family of 4. So, you can use less items accordingly. Rather than white sugar, we've incorporated brown sugar and honey. We have alos included the option of adding mint leaf.

Ingredients

  • 1 Gallon: Water
  • 10 Large: Lemons
  • 1 Cup: Brown Sugar
  • ¼ Cup: 100% Honey
  • (Optional) 8 Sprigs: Mint Leaf
Steps
  1. Cut your lemons and harvest the juice
  2. Bring water to a low simmer and step the mint leaves for 5 minutes.
  3. Remove the water from heat, extract the leaves from the water and slowly add brown sugar and honey while simutanuosly stiring.
  4. Allow the sugar water mixure to cool for 10 minutes
  5. Add you lemon juice into the water solution
  6. Enjoy!!
